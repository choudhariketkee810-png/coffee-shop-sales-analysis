# Coffee Shop Sales – SQL Analysis Insights

## Key Findings
- **Top-performing store:** Hell's Kitchen generated the highest revenue ($236,511.17).
- **Top product category:** Coffee generated the highest revenue ($269,952.45).
- **Top revenue-generating product:** Sustainably Grown Organic Lg generated $21,151.75 in revenue.
- **Peak store/hour combination:** Hell's Kitchen at approximately 10:00 had the highest hourly revenue ($33,605.81).
- Revenue was calculated as **transaction_qty × unit_price**.

## Business Insights
1. Focus staffing and inventory planning around the highest-revenue hours.
2. Maintain strong availability of the leading product categories and products.
3. Compare store-level performance to identify locations requiring operational or promotional support.
4. Use hourly sales patterns to optimize staffing and reduce service delays during peak periods.
5. Use product-level sales and revenue together: high quantity does not always mean high revenue.
